## 1. Main setups and entry triggers (per trader)

### Takeaway
Nearly the whole cohort trades the same three families of setups, all descending from Pradeep Bonde and Qullamaggie:
- (a) breakouts from tight bases after a prior big move, often entered on an opening-range-high (ORH) break;
- (b) episodic pivots (EPs), meaning gap-ups on news or earnings;
- (c) pullbacks or reclaims of short EMAs (9/10/21) and AVWAP. Kell's "cycle of price action" (wedge pop / EMA crossback) is the main EMA-reclaim variant.

Parabolic shorts are a secondary setup for Qullamaggie and Marios. Ty Rajnus is an outlier: he is a short-only, systematic intraday trader in micro-caps.

### Cited Findings

- The pullback-entry framing ("low-risk, high-reward entries") also appears in — [Financial Wisdom TV](https://www.financialwisdomtv.com/post/martin-luk-s-pullback-trading-strategy-how-the-2025-u-s-investing-champion-finds-low-risk-high-re) [secondary]

## 2. Initial stop placement and stop-size rules ("short stop")

### Takeaway
The cohort agrees on the rule. The stop goes at the **low of the entry day (LOD)**, or at the low of an intraday candle or pivot, and it is **capped by volatility**: no wider than about 1 ADR/ATR, and ideally ≤0.5 ADR for Luk and Marios. Traders **skip the trade if the stop would be too wide**. Typical stops are about 1–5% from entry.

### Cited Findings
- **Martin Luk:**
  - Stops are tight, "typically less than 3%." [secondary] — [podwise episode notes](https://podwise.ai/episodes/7557502)
  - The stop is never more than half the ADR (e.g., a 5% ADR stock gets a stop of about 2.5% or less). [secondary] — [tradepiko](https://www.tradepiko.com/blog/martin-luk-trading-strategy)
  - The stop goes at the low of the entry day. If that is more than 5% away, he uses the low of the 5-minute entry candle instead. [secondary] — [tradepiko](https://www.tradepiko.com/blog/martin-luk-trading-strategy)
  - On the AVWAP/EMA flush setup, the stop goes "below the day's low." [secondary] — [Finetiq](https://ideas.finetiqsystems.com/en/ideas/avwap-ema-flush/)
- **Qullamaggie:**
  - The stop goes at the low of the day, "not wider than ATR/ADR." [secondary] — [TradingView Breakout V2 description](https://www.tradingview.com/script/5bTajWQM-Qullamaggie-Breakout-V2/?amp)
  - One write-up gives the cap as 1 ADR on breakouts and 1.5x ADR on EPs. [secondary] — [sovascan](https://sovascan.com/learn/qullamaggie-trading-strategy/) / [Stage2Stocks](https://www.stage2stocks.com/learn/qullamaggie) (search extract; exact page attribution uncertain)
  - Stream notes say he "usually" uses LOD, but sometimes a higher stop, e.g., on earnings plays. [secondary] — [Retail Trader's Repository stream notes](https://retailtradersrepository.substack.com/p/kristjan-kullamagi-qullamaggie-stream-092)
  - A planner tool built on his method uses ADR stops. [secondary tool] — [sharepredictions planner](https://sharepredictions.com/tools/qullamaggie-breakout-planner/)
- **Marios Stamatoudis:**
  - Entry minus stop should be under 1x ATR, and under 0.5 ATR is better. If the stop is wider than 1 ATR, the trade is "generally not worth taking." He does not use a universal % stop. [secondary, interview notes] — [Retail Trader's Repository: TraderLion podcast notes](https://retailtradersrepository.substack.com/p/marios-stamatoudis-the-traderlion); [TraderLion conference notes](https://retailtradersrepository.substack.com/p/marios-stamatoudis-traderlionconference2024-p1)
  - Breakouts get a stop at LOD. Parabolic shorts get a stop at the high of day or the high of the entry candle. [secondary] — same sources
- **Jeff Sun:**
  - "3 stops at 33% level to final stop (usually LoD), 33% net size at each level," set on Day 0. [primary X] — [X post](https://x.com/jfsrev/status/1841852289742733701?lang=en)
  - He keeps the staggered stops through T+3, and keeps "some size at LoD for price action to develop… max loss is 0.7R after cost." [primary X] — [X post](https://x.com/jfsrev/status/1944314273146318875)
  - Staggering the stops cuts his average loss "substantially from -1R to -0.7R." [primary X] — [X post](https://x.com/jfsrev/status/1805517143968227506)
  - The purpose is "to reduce your losses well before a trade hits the full stop—ideally keeping them far below -1R." [primary X] — [X post](https://x.com/jfsrev/status/1937402718278369337)
- **Oliver Kell:**
  - The stop goes just below the pivot or price bars, or "below the near-term price consolidation" on an EMA reclaim. He raises it as the stock rises. [secondary] — [TraderLion EMA Crossback](https://traderlion.com/technical-analysis/chart-patterns/ema-crossback/); [TraderLion lesson](https://traderlion.com/?p=418625)
  - He starts with about a 15% position and a tight stop. [secondary interview] — [stockbsessed substack interview](https://stockbsessed.substack.com/p/oliver-kell-interview-the-mind-and)
- **Pradeep Bonde:**
  - The EP stop goes under the low of the entry day. [secondary] — [therighttrader](https://therighttrader.com/learn/trading-strategies/episodic-pivot)
  - For momentum bursts, "buy the day the color changes, stop at that day's low, hold 3–5 days." [secondary, unverified] — [OKX insight](https://www.okx.com/en-us/orbit/insight/93635992718976)
  - A document's table of contents lists "3 STOP rules," with different stops for EPs and for stocks in play. [unverified; doc not readable] — [Google doc](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1a4-A2OQ2PO_R7JrczbmlVrWT3XePC7_B/export?format=pdf)
- **Richard Moglen:**
  - Stops are typically 1–2%. [secondary blog] — [wonkmonksnotes](https://wonkmonksnotes.wordpress.com/2025/09/10/trading-richard-moglen/)
  - The breakout stop is the LOD [secondary] — [videohighlight](https://videohighlight.com/v/0Vpt3P2ATPQ?mediaType=youtube&language=en&summaryType=default&aiFormatted=false)
  - On the EMA reversal short, the stop is the high of day, about 1% risk. — [TraderLion Trade Lab](https://traderlion.com/profile/richard-moglen/the-ema-reversal-short-trade-setup-trade-lab-educational-article/)
- **Ty Rajnus (the exception):** He uses "backtested max drawdown sizing." Each loss is larger in % terms, but a high win rate compensates. [secondary] — [TraderLion](https://traderlion.com/profile/ty-rajnus/ty-rajnus-returned-382/)

### Inferences
- Combining an LOD stop with an ADR/ATR cap produces a de-facto "skip if stop too wide" filter. Luk and Marios state it explicitly (above 0.5 ADR, or above 1 ATR, means no trade). Qullamaggie applies it implicitly through the ADR cap.
- Jeff Sun's 3-tier stop is a refinement that shrinks the average loss below 1R. Luk's fallback to the 5-minute candle low and Marios's 0.5 ATR preference do the same job. The cohort is actively engineering *smaller than 1R* average losses.

- Bonde's "3 stop rules" document could not be opened.

## 3. Profit taking and trailing ("long profit")

### Takeaway
The common template has three steps:
1. Sell 1/4–1/2 into early strength, either after 3–5 days or at about 2–3x ADR / 2R+.
2. Move the stop to breakeven.
3. Trail the remainder with a close below a short MA: 9/10 EMA for fast names, 20/21 EMA for slower trends, sometimes 50.

Kell and Jeff Sun also sell into "extension" measured from the 10 EMA or 50-MA.

### Cited Findings
- **Qullamaggie:**
  - Sell 1/3–1/2 after 3–5 days, move the stop to breakeven, and trail the rest with the 10 or 20-day MA. [secondary] — [TradingView Breakout V2](https://www.tradingview.com/script/5bTajWQM-Qullamaggie-Breakout-V2/?amp); [swingly pre-market report](https://swingly.beehiiv.com/p/pre-market-report-16th-april-2024)
  - 2019 stream notes give a staged scheme: sell 1/3–1/4 on the first close below the 10-day, more on a close below the 20-day, and the rest on a close below the 50-day (with exceptions for higher lows). [secondary] — [Retail Trader's Repository](https://retailtradersrepository.substack.com/p/kristjan-kullamagi-qullamaggie-stream-092)
  - The two descriptions conflict on the fraction sold and on the "3–5 day" timing. [noted conflict]
- **Martin Luk:**
  - He usually trails on the 9 EMA, using the 21 or 50 EMA in strong trends. [secondary] — [Finetiq](https://ideas.finetiqsystems.com/en/ideas/avwap-ema-flush/)
  - He rides trends with the 9/21 EMA but sometimes sells into strength when the market is extended. [primary FAQ via extract] — [martinluk.com FAQ](https://martinluk.com/faq/)
  - Example: on SOFI he trimmed at 6R and exited on a weak close at the 9 EMA. [secondary] — [podwise](https://podwise.ai/episodes/3678850)
  - I found no general rule for trim size or timing.
- **Oliver Kell:**
  - He takes profits at the second "exhaustion extension" from the 10 EMA, because reversal odds rise with each extension. [secondary] — [TradingView Kell cycle](https://in.tradingview.com/script/xHyBTcMq-Oliver-Kell-Cycle-of-Price-Action/); [TraderLion MU trade](https://traderlion.com/profile/oliver-kell/mu-wedge-pop/)
  - He trails at the 10/21 EMA or bar lows. A daily close below the 10 EMA means trim or exit on aggressive trades. [secondary, lower reliability] — [stratcraft infographic](https://stratcraft.ai/oliver-kell-ema); [tldl notes](https://tldl.club/en/noted/683d80f5a070c61689f6ac62)
  - On weekly charts, he sells into extensions from the 10-week EMA. [secondary] — [tldl notes](https://tldl.club/en/noted/683d80f5a070c61689f6ac62)
- **Marios Stamatoudis:**
  - He sells a fraction at 2.5–3x ADR, moves the stop to breakeven, and trails the rest with MAs. [secondary] — [Retail Trader's Repository](https://retailtradersrepository.substack.com/p/marios-stamatoudis-the-traderlion); [financialwisdomtv](https://www.financialwisdomtv.com/post/the-winning-stock-trading-strategy-from-the-2023-us-trading-champion)
  - He does not update the stop unless he has taken a partial. After the first partial, the stop moves to breakeven, or he splits the shares into two parts. [secondary] — [Retail Trader's Repository conf. notes](https://retailtradersrepository.substack.com/p/marios-stamatoudis-traderlionconference2024-p1)
- **Jeff Sun:**
  - If profit-to-risk exceeds 2x on days 0–2, he sells 33%. [primary X] — [X post](https://x.com/jfsrev/status/1841852289742733701?lang=en)
  - He sells a further 33% when price is about 10x ATR extended from the 50-MA. Beyond day 4, at 8–10x ATR extension, he sells another 33%. [primary X + secondary] — [X post](https://x.com/jfsrev/status/2030681452455883001); [Trading Resource Hub](https://tradingresourcehub.substack.com/p/risk-and-position-management-jeff-sun)
- **Pradeep Bonde:**
  - Momentum bursts are held about 3–5 days. Exit rules vary by source: partials on day 2–3, or exit on the first down day after 3+ up days. [secondary] — [Stage2Stocks](https://www.stage2stocks.com/learn/stockbee); [TradingView Stockbee Momentum Pro](https://in.tradingview.com/script/njlDpagc-Pradeep-Bonde-Stockbee-Momentum-Pro/)
  - For EPs, move the stop only after "a natural reaction." Sell half after the third leg, and the rest if it goes parabolic or breaks the trendline. [secondary, TraderLion 2024 talk notes] — [TraderLion podcast page](https://traderlion.com/podcast/pradeep-bonde-episodic-pivots/)
  - Bonde: "I move my stops as position becomes profitable. Sometime I will move stop multiple times in a day." [primary blog comment via extract] — [Stockbee blog](https://stockbee.blogspot.com/2014/07/my-swing-trading-process-flow.html?m=0)
- **Richard Moglen:** He moves the stop to cost once the stock is up 2x the initial risk. [primary X via extract] — [Moglen on X](https://x.com/RichardMoglen/status/1518220026347548672) (extract attribution uncertain)
- **TraderLion house guidance (Moglen's firm, general):** Move to breakeven once up 5–10%. Sell part at about 1.5R, and trail with the 10/20-day MA or swing lows. — [TraderLion stop-loss placement](https://traderlion.com/risk-management/stop-loss-placement/)

### Inferences
- "Short stop, long profit" works through asymmetric management. The initial risk is fixed and tiny, and the partial-plus-breakeven step turns most trades that survive the first days into free rolls. The remaining position runs until a *closing* break of a short EMA, which lets the 10R–30R outliers develop.
- Speed sets the MA choice. Fast, high-ADR names get the 9/10 EMA, and slower leaders get the 20/21 or 50.

### Gaps
- No primary, quantified partial-sell rule from Luk or Kell was found.

## 4. Position sizing, risk per trade, exposure, drawdown behavior

### Takeaway
Risk per trade is very small, about **0.25%–0.5% of equity** (1% max). Because stops are tight, **position sizes are large**, roughly 10–35% of the account, with margin up to 120–280% total exposure for Kell and Luk. Every trader cuts size during drawdowns and scales up when the equity curve is rising.

### Cited Findings
- **Martin Luk:**
  - He risked about 0.5% of equity per trade in 2024–25. [secondary] — [tradepiko](https://www.tradepiko.com/blog/martin-luk-trading-strategy)
  - Total margin exposure goes up to 280%. [secondary] — [completetradersedge](https://completetradersedge.com/martin-luk-us-investing-championship/)
  - His FAQ says that if learning again, he'd use **0.3%** risk, because his tight-stop, low-win-rate style produces losing streaks. With a 0.5% stop he "might still use around 25% size." [primary FAQ via extract] — [martinluk.com FAQ](https://martinluk.com/faq/)
  - He reduces exposure in drawdowns and increases it in favorable equity-curve phases. A 2024 journal review changed his sizing. [secondary] — [completetradersedge](https://completetradersedge.com/martin-luk-us-investing-championship/); [Trading Resource Hub](https://tradingresourcehub.substack.com/p/martin-luk-283-usic-2024-key-lessons)
  - A podcast summary claims "5% per trade." This conflicts with all other sources and is likely an error. [unverified] — [podwise](https://podwise.ai/episodes/7557502)
- **Qullamaggie:**
  - Risk is about 0.25–1% per trade (0.3–0.5% typical, 1% max). [secondary, conflicting] — [snappchart](https://www.snappchart.app/blog/beginner-playbook/qullamaggie-trading-strategy); [completetradersedge](https://completetradersedge.com/kristjan-kullamagi-qullamaggie/)
  - Position size is 10–20% of the account, and some cite a 30% overnight cap. [secondary] — [snappchart](https://www.snappchart.app/blog/beginner-playbook/qullamaggie-trading-strategy)
  - His account reportedly peaked around $105M and then drew down about $60M. [secondary] — [completetradersedge](https://completetradersedge.com/kristjan-kullamagi-qullamaggie/)
- **Oliver Kell:**
  - He starts with about 15% and adds about 15% if the stock follows through the next day. [secondary interview] — [stockbsessed](https://stockbsessed.substack.com/p/oliver-kell-interview-the-mind-and)
  - His book says 25–35% goes to a top idea on margin, with total exposure of about 120–150%. [secondary, book summary] — [EBC](https://www.ebc.com/forex/oliver-kell-trading-strategy)
  - He buys in stages, raising both the average price and the stop. [secondary] — [note.com summary](https://note.com/natty_finch1027/n/n4d3cb55e5660?hl=en)
- **Marios Stamatoudis:** Risk is about 0.25–0.4% per trade (another source says 0.2–0.3%). When adding, he puts the stop above the new average price. [secondary] — [Retail Trader's Repository](https://retailtradersrepository.substack.com/p/marios-stamatoudis-the-traderlion); [stocksandfuturestrading](https://stocksandfuturestrading.com/marios-stamatoudis-trading-strategy/)
- **Jeff Sun:** Risk is about 0.42% per trade. [secondary] — [tradepiko blog index](https://www.tradepiko.com/blog)
- **Richard Moglen:** Sources give 0.5% [secondary blog](https://wonkmonksnotes.wordpress.com/2025/09/10/trading-richard-moglen/); under 1% [his substack](https://tradingengineered.substack.com/p/5-keys-to-master-trading-psychology); and up to 1.25% for a normal position [study notes](https://studylib.net/doc/25670232/market-chat-with-shahid-saleem-and-richard-moglen). The variation is probably by period.
- **Ty Rajnus:** He uses compounding position sizing with a 5% monthly drawdown rule. [secondary, podcast listing] — [TraderLion](https://traderlion.com/profile/ty-rajnus/ty-rajnus-returned-382/)

### Inferences
- Worked example: 0.5% risk with a 2% stop gives a 25% position. That is exactly Luk's stated combination. Tight stops are what allow concentrated, margin-levered exposure while losses stay tiny per trade.
- Drawdown protocols (smaller risk, 0.3%, monthly drawdown limits) exist because 20–35% win rates produce long losing streaks.

- Qullamaggie's own sizing statements were seen only through secondary pages.

## 5. Win rate, R profile, competition records and verification

### Takeaway
The long-side swing traders have **low win rates, about 23–35%**, and **large average win/loss ratios, about 5:1**, with outliers of 10–30R. Rajnus is the exception, with a 66% win rate on shorts. The USIC results are publicly announced via BusinessWire press releases. USIC uses broker statements, but independent audit detail was not confirmed.

### Cited Findings
- **Martin Luk:**
  - 2024 results: 104 winners and 319 losers, a 23% win rate, average win about 15% vs. average loss about 3%. [secondary] — [Trading Resource Hub](https://tradingresourcehub.substack.com/p/martin-luk-283-usic-2024-key-lessons)
  - The longest losing streak was 18 trades in one source and 33 in another (different periods). [secondary, conflicting] — [Trading Resource Hub](https://tradingresourcehub.substack.com/p/martin-luk-283-usic-2024-key-lessons); [James Muninn on X](https://x.com/Muninn/status/2117629160348668285)

## 6. Study and review process; common principles

### Takeaway
The cohort learns through **model books** (Bonde, Marios's "reverse engineering" of past winners), **daily scans** (4% / EP / ADR scans), **trade journals with statistics** (Luk, Jeff Sun's 8-year datasets), and **public streams/podcasts** (Qullamaggie's streams, TraderLion). The shared principles are a tight technical stop capped by ADR, small fixed % risk, concentrated size, early partials plus breakeven, an EMA trail for outliers, and acceptance of a low win rate.

### Inferences
These are the common principles across the cohort:
1. Stops are structural, at LOD or an intraday pivot, not a fixed %.
2. Stops are volatility-capped at ≤1 ADR/ATR, ideally ≤0.5. Trades are skipped otherwise.
3. Risk is 0.25–0.5% per trade.
4. Size is backed out from the stop, giving large positions.
5. Partials come early (days 3–5, or 2–3 ADR / 2R) and the stop moves to breakeven.
6. The runner trails on a closing break of the 9/10/20/21 EMA.
7. A low win rate is accepted in exchange for 5:1+ payoff.
8. Size is cut in drawdowns.
9. Journals and statistics drive rule changes.
